Game Synopsis / Summary
Rainbow’s End is a single-screen, dual stick tactical shooter set in a magical diorama
world.​
You play as a leprechaun tasked with turning rainbow magic into gold by pushing
enchanted pots into the unicorn’s rainbow trails, then defending them from waves of
clever adversaries.​
Enemies become tougher and smarter as you score, while you use a mix of
weapons, fae spells, whimsical traps, and quick thinking to survive the chaos.​
The game fuses the crisp combat and line-of-sight tactics of top-down shooters
(Bullet Echo) with whimsical, readable art, emergent escalation, and a strong focus
on tactical cover, resource management, and player skill.

Core Gameplay Loop
Map & Procedural Setup
●​ Arena contains:
●​ 1 Fae Tree (where the pots are banked)
●​ 3 empty pots
●​ 1 unicorn
●​ 1 player (leprechaun)
●​ Environment:
●​ Cover: Rocks/logs (blocks LoS/movement; used by AI/Player)
●​ Concealment: Bushes & tall grass (hide vision only, can walk through;
player is transparent to signal invisibility) firing within concealment
shows player for .75s.

Pot, Rainbow Snake & Siphon System
●​ Unicorn leaves a timestamped rainbow floating trail.
●​ Placing a pot on the rainbow breaks the unicorn’s trail:
●​ The pot begins harvesting gold based on rainbow’s timestamp
●​ Shortly after a new rainbow trail starts behind the Unicorn
●​ Rainbow trail (Sky-rainbow VFX) rises from pot into the air and stays
while pot is unmoved
●​ Up to 3 simultaneous pots/sky-rainbows harvesting at once
●​ exponential gold harvest: 1 Pot: 1x gold | 2 Pots: 2.5x gold | 3 Pots: 5x gold.
●​ moving a harvesting pot dissipates the sky-rainbow and player forfeits
remaining harvest gold.

Weapons & Combat
Weapons (Bullet Echo-inspired)
●​ Bow & Arrow (infinite ammo, refills via exiting bushes, fast RoF/narrow cone)
●​ Pistol (Police drop, 12 shots, standard-sized Cone, quick pickup)
●​ Shotgun (Police/Military drop, 8 shots, short & wide cone, Slow RoF)
●​ Assault Rifle (Military drop, 30 shots, rapid RoF, medium pickup)
●​ Player’s movement increase cone of fire, player must be still to aim (reduce
cone narrowness)
●​ Tracer projectiles, dynamic VFX/SFX, auto-revert to bow when out of ammo

Hybrid HP and Staged Feedback
(applies to player and enemies)
●​ All mobs and player have HP
●​ +67%: no effect/visual flinch
●​ ≤67%: Green flash/flinch/cone of fire increase
●​ ≤33%: Yellow flash/flinch/knockback
●​ ≤ 10%: Red flash/“Downed” KO animation/VFX
●​ Each hit = instant VFX pop/splash of blood
●​ Mob downed state = 2–3s dramatic KO before fadeout.
●​ Leprechaun can not be killed, at 0hp is down for 3s

5. Spells & Traps
Spells (Active, Magic Stance only, Cooldown)
Default spell (wether holding pot of gold or not), player can create two portals. After 2nd
portal, oldest portal removed for new portal created. Only Unicorn, player, pot of gold can go
through the portal.

●​ Spriggan’s Gust: AoE wind, knockback
●​ Lucky Clover Ring: lures unicorn
●​ Rainbow Orb: Color unlocks over time:​
Red: Burn, Orange: Confuse, Yellow: Gold drain/magnet, Green: Root, Blue:
Freeze, Indigo: Blind, Violet: Sleep

Traps

AI, Escalation, & Cover Tactics
●​ Travellers seek out pots of gold to steal gold, reducing player’s gold.
●​ Scoops from pot of gold, fills up a sack, and walks off the map/screen
●​ When killed gold drops for player to pick up
●​ Can pick up dropped weapons.
●​ Add a clear VFX/SFX to show Traveller is draining the pot! (-X amount
of gold!”) so players FEEL the loss.​
●​ Police patrol the map/screen.
●​ Uses cover
●​ killing may spawn Military and weapon (pistol/shotgun), or ammo
●​ Military: Uses cover, they seek out and hide behind logs/rocks, flank,
suppress.
●​ If 2 or more military on the map, will seek out pot of gold to take off
screen.
●​ Killing may spawn weapon (pistol/shotgun, AR), or ammo
●​ Bushes/tall grass provide concealment for player and bots
●​ Unicorn is never blocked. Unicorn rainbow trail reveals concealed, and
unicorn hops over rocks and logs,
●​ Mob Spawn Logic: One at opposite side of the player, one at opposite side of
the mob. Enemy type depends on last killed.
●​ Goal: Mobs escalate with player aggression, not just time

Pot & Unicorn Relationship
●​ On collision, unicron destroys pot (animated rainbow & gold explosion).
●​ Unicorn moves weighted randomly
